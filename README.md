# SoundStick — WhatsApp Sound Stickers

**Make stickers that talk.** SoundStick lets you turn your photos and videos into stickers, attach a sound to each one, and share them with friends.

## Features

- **Create a sticker from a photo** — pick any photo, it is cropped square and ready to go, with an optional caption written on the sticker.
- **Create a sticker from a video** — SoundStick grabs a frame from the middle of the video as the sticker image.
- **Five ways to add a sound:**
  - Record your own voice (up to 10 seconds)
  - Pick any audio file from your phone
  - Built-in sound effects: Pop, Ding, Drumroll, Buzzer, Whistle and Boing
  - Text-to-Speech: type it, SoundStick says it
  - Use the video's own sound when the sticker came from a video
- **Tap-to-play preview** — tap the sticker and it bounces while its sound plays.
- **Sticker packs** — organize stickers into packs (WhatsApp needs 3–30 stickers per pack).
- **Add to WhatsApp** — send a pack straight into WhatsApp as a sticker pack. WhatsApp sticker packs are images only, so stickers are silent inside WhatsApp itself.
- **Share with Sound** — share any sticker *with* its sound as a short video that plays in WhatsApp and other apps.
- Bold black & yellow design, dark theme throughout.

## How to use

1. Tap **Create Sound Sticker**.
2. Pick a **photo** or a **video**.
3. Add an optional caption.
4. Add **one sound** using any of the sound buttons.
5. Tap the sticker to preview it with its sound.
6. Tap **Save Sticker to Pack** and choose a pack (or make a new one).
7. Open the pack and tap **Add to WhatsApp**, or tap **Share with Sound** on any sticker to send it as a video with sound.
