# SoundStick — WhatsApp Sound Stickers

**Make stickers that talk.** SoundStick lets you turn your photos and videos into stickers, attach a sound to each one, and share them with friends.

## Features

- **Create a sticker from a photo** — pick any photo, it is cropped square and ready to go, with an optional caption written on the sticker.
- **Create an ANIMATED sticker from a video** — pick a video, trim the part you want (start + 1s / 2s / 3s), crop it with zoom and move controls, and SoundStick turns it into a real animated WebP sticker that moves in WhatsApp.
- **Five ways to add a sound:**
  - Record your own voice (up to 10 seconds)
  - Pick any audio file from your phone
  - Built-in sound effects: Pop, Ding, Drumroll, Buzzer, Whistle and Boing
  - Text-to-Speech: type it, SoundStick says it
  - Use the video's own sound when the sticker came from a video
- **Tap-to-play preview** — tap the sticker and it bounces while its sound plays.
- **Sticker packs** — organize stickers into packs (WhatsApp needs 3–30 stickers per pack; a pack is all-static or all-animated).
- **Add to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business** — send a pack straight into either app as a sticker pack, right from the pack screen or straight after creating a sticker. WhatsApp sticker packs are images only, so stickers are silent inside WhatsApp itself.
- **Share with Sound** — share any sticker *with* its sound as a short video that plays in WhatsApp and other apps.
- **Community** — publish your stickers so every SoundStick user can see and use them, and browse the Community feed to grab stickers other people made (tap to hear them, "Use this sticker" to add them to your packs).
- Bold black & yellow design, dark theme throughout.

## How to use

1. Tap **Create Sound Sticker**.
2. Pick a **photo** (static sticker) or a **video** (animated sticker — trim & crop it first).
3. Add an optional caption.
4. Add **one sound** using any of the sound buttons.
5. Tap the sticker to preview it with its sound.
6. Tap **Save Sticker to Pack** and choose a pack (or make a new one).
7. Open the pack and tap **Add to WhatsApp** or **Add to WhatsApp Business**, tap **Share with Sound** on any sticker to send it as a video with sound, or **Publish to Community** to share it with all SoundStick users.

## Privacy

SoundStick has no account. Your stickers, sounds and recordings stay on your phone — the only exception is stickers you choose to **Publish to Community**, which are uploaded so other SoundStick users can see and use them. See [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
